A QUICK GUIDE

Travel planning, made simple.

Start with a trip. Add the details as you go.

1. Plan a trip

Select Plan a trip. Enter your city and country, travel dates, and total budget in Philippine pesos. Save to open your trip.

2. Confirm the location

On the trip page, choose Destination & weather. Search for your city and select the correct result. This saves the location so the weather tool knows which place to use.

3. Explore before you go

Check weather for trip dates within the next 3 days, read about your destination, or convert your PHP budget into another currency. Currency conversion is an estimate; it does not change your saved budget.

4. Record what you spend

Use Add an expense on your trip page. Enter what you paid for, the amount in PHP, and the payment date. Your remaining budget updates automatically. Remove an incorrect entry and add it again.

5. Keep your photos

Choose Photos on your trip page. Select a JPEG, PNG, or WebP photo up to 5 MB, add an optional caption, and save it. You’ll see a preview before uploading.

Find and update a trip

Use Search your trips and the status filter on My trips. Open a trip and select Edit trip to change its dates, destination, notes, or budget. Changing the destination means choosing its location again.

If something is unavailable

Your trip and budget still work when a travel service is unavailable. Try the tool again later. If location search or photos remain unavailable, ask the site owner to check the service setup.
